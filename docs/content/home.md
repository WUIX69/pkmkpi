## Home
The national federation of Persons With Disability in the Philippines
**Pambansang Kapisanan ng mga May Kapansanan ng Pilipinas, Inc.** (PKMKPI) is a non-stock, non-profit people’s organization registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission under SEC Reg. No. CN202105447. It is the only National Federation (Kapisanan) of Persons With Disability in the Philippines, composed of corporate members — local federations and organizations — from all seventeen regions of the country.

PKMKPI is an organization of harnessed Persons With Disability (PWD) and Persons with Work Related Disability (PWRD) across ten (10) types of disability, bonding as friends and relating as one big family — with respect for who, what, how and where they are.

### Our goals: AEIOU
This Kapisanan is the alliance of harnessed PWDs and PWRDs, initially composed of forty (40) corporate members covering seventeen regions in the Philippines, whose goals are AEIOU:
AAwareness
EEmpowerment
IInvolvement
OCooperation
UUnity
Together these describe the awareness, empowerment, involvement, cooperation and unity of the PWD sector in the whole country.
### Stand with the PWD sector
Your support helps us advocate for the full implementation of disability laws and build self-reliant PWD communities nationwide.
