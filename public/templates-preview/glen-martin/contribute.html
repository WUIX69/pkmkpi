Every contribution builds a more inclusive Philippines

PKMKPI is a non-stock, non-profit organization registered under SEC Reg. No. CN202105447.

Cash donations

Cash contributions support advocacy, capacity-building seminars, regional assemblies, and assistance for members in need.

In-kind donations

Assistive devices, wheelchairs, hearing aids, medicines, food packs, school supplies, and livelihood materials are welcome.

Corporate giving

Companies can sponsor livelihood and employment programs, accessibility projects, or specific regional activities.
