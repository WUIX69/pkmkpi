WHO WE ARE

About PKMKPI

A non-stock, non-profit national organization uniting Persons With Disability (PWD) and Persons with Work Related Disability (PWRD) across ten types of disability — bonded as one family, respected in who, what, how, and where they are.

Registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission under SEC Reg. No. CN202105447, PKMKPI is the only National Federation (Kapisanan) of Persons With Disability in the Philippines — an alliance initially composed of forty (40) corporate members, local federations and organizations, covering all seventeen regions of the country.

Vision

A society where persons with disability are treated with equality, dignity, and respect for their expressed choices — with equal opportunities to participate in a society where abilities, rather than disabilities, are recognized.

Mission

To advocate for the full implementation of disability laws.

Objective

To attain an empowered, self-sufficient, self-reliant persons with disability.
