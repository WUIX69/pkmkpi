Tungkol sa Kapisanan

Bonding as friends, relating as one big family

Pambansang Kapisanan ng mga May Kapansanan ng Pilipinas, Inc. (PKMKPI) is a non-stock, non-profit people’s organization registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission under SEC Reg. No. CN202105447. It is the only National Federation (Kapisanan) of Persons With Disability in the Philippines, composed of corporate members (local federations and organizations) from all seventeen regions of the country.

PKMKPI is an organization of harnessed Persons With Disability (PWD) and Persons with Work Related Disability (PWRD) across ten (10) types of disability, bonding as friends and relating as one big family, with respect for who, what, how and where they are.

“Isang bansa, isang Kapisanan, isang layunin. Sama-sama nating itataguyod ang karapatan ng bawat Pilipinong may kapansanan.”

Fe V. Corpuz, D.D.

Estratehikong pillar

Our goals: AEIOU

Five strategic pillars guide the work of the Kapisanan and its corporate members across the country.

A

Aksyon

Empowerment in action: advocacy, rights education, and campaigns that end stigma.

E

Edukasyon

Inclusive education and skills: training, assistive technology, and legal literacy.

I

Integrasyon

Social and economic inclusion: representation from the barangay to Congress.

O

Oportunidad

Livelihood and careers: adaptive livelihood, employment, and self-reliance.

U

Ugnayan

Cross-sector partnerships: resilient networks across all seventeen regions.
