About Pambansang Kapisanan ng mga May Kapansanan ng Pilipinas, Inc.

PKMKPI is an alliance initially composed of forty (40) corporate members — local federations and organizations — covering all seventeen regions of the country. It is an organization of harnessed Persons With Disability (PWD) and Persons with Work Related Disability (PWRD) in ten (10) types of disability, bonding as friends and relating as one big family with respect of who, what, how and where they are.

Vision

A society where PWDs are treated with equality, dignity, and respect, with abilities recognized over disabilities.

Mission

To advocate relentlessly for the full implementation of disability laws and inclusive social safety nets nationwide.

Objective

To attain an empowered, self-sufficient, and self-reliant community of persons with disability.

Framework For Impact

Our Goals: A · E · I · O · U

This Kapisanan is the alliance initially composed of forty corporate members across seventeen regions whose unified goals are AEIOU:

A

Awareness

Educating communities, families, and government on disability rights, accessibility laws, and equal opportunities.

E

Empowerment

Equipping PWDs with technical skills, livelihood training, and leadership capabilities for economic self-reliance.

I

Involvement

Ensuring active participation of persons with disability in local policy formulation, civic planning, and elections.

O

Cooperation

Fostering inter-agency collaboration between civil society, LGUs, corporate allies, and national agencies.

U

Unity

Solidifying the collective voice of all 10 disability classifications and 17 regions as one national family.
