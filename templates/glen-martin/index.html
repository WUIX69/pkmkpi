WHO WE ARE

Building a more inclusive Philippines

PKMKPI is a non-stock, non-profit national federation of Persons With Disability and Persons with Work Related Disability, composed of corporate members from all seventeen regions of the country.

OUR REACH

One national federation. One shared purpose.

OUR GOALS

AEIOU

Our work is guided by awareness, empowerment, involvement, cooperation, and unity.

A

Awareness

Build understanding of disability rights and inclusion.

E

Empowerment

Strengthen the capacity and self-reliance of PWD communities.

I

Involvement

Ensure meaningful participation in decisions that affect PWDs.

O

Cooperation

Work with government, organizations, companies, and communities.

U

Unity

Build a stronger national movement through solidarity.
