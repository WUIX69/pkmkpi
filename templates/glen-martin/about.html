ABOUT PKMKPI

Who we are and what we stand for

A national federation united around representation, empowerment, and inclusion for Persons With Disability and Persons with Work Related Disability.

OUR STORY

A stronger voice through national unity.

Pambansang Kapisanan ng mga May Kapansanan ng Pilipinas, Inc. (PKMKPI) is a non-stock, non-profit national organization of PWD/PWRD. It is registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission under SEC Reg. No. CN202105447 and is composed of corporate members from all seventeen regions.

Its purpose is to strengthen the representation, participation, and empowerment of the PWD sector while building partnerships for an inclusive Philippines.

Vision

A society where Persons With Disability are empowered, self-sufficient, self-reliant, and fully included.

Mission

Unite and empower PWD organizations through advocacy, capacity building, cooperation, and meaningful participation.

Objective

Promote the rights, welfare, dignity, and opportunities of Persons With Disability.

CORE VALUES
