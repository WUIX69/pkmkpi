Ways you can stand with the PWD sector

PKMKPI is powered by members and friends who believe in an empowered, self-sufficient, and self-reliant PWD sector.

Become a member

Local PWD federations and organizations may apply as corporate members and join a nationwide network across all 17 regions.

Volunteer

Share your time and skills in advocacy, capacity-building activities, livelihood programs, and community outreach.

Partner with us

Government agencies, LGUs, companies, and civic groups can partner with us on inclusive programs and employment opportunities.

Advocate

Help push for the full implementation of disability laws and spread the message: Nothing About Us Without Us.
